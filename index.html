CRITICAL POSITION // 01

DISMANTLING THE CONSERVATORY WALLS

Trained as a classical virtuoso at the Hanns Eisler School of Music in Berlin, Voss abandoned orthodox concert decorum following an epiphany inside the cavernous concrete belly of Kraftwerk Berlin in 2014.

Rather than treating the concert grand piano as a museum artifact, Voss treats the instrument as a colossal percussion resonator. By placing custom contact transducers directly on the iron frame, dampening the copper bass strings with lead blocks, and routing acoustic resonance through arrays of custom analog Eurorack filters, Voss discovered an unheard continuum: the point where Chopin dissolves into raw seismic vibration.
