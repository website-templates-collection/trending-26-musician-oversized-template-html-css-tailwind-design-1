## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an avant-garde editorial identity tailored for high-concept sonic artists operating at the collision of acoustic neoclassical composition and visceral electronic synthesis. The interface merges stark European editorial design with raw, calculated brutalism: monumental headline scale, architectural spatial boundaries, razor-sharp technical metadata, and visceral color shocks.

The personality balances high-art austerity with underground energy. It communicates structural discipline, uncompromising artistic vision, and atmospheric immersion. Every view functions as an oversized poster or gallery monograph rather than a conventional promotional website. 

Key stylistic principles:
- **Typographic Scale as Architecture:** Giant scale ratios where typography itself forms textures, background silhouettes, and layout containers.
- **Editorial Brutalism:** Monolithic structural grids, hairline dividers (0.5px to 1px), sharp rectangular boundaries, and raw monospaced index tagging.
- **Kinetic Tension:** Stark spatial voids interrupted by sudden saturated electric accents and kinetic audio cues.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for severe visual impact and high legibility against dark staging environments. The foundational surface is not standard digital dark gray, but a deep obsidian coal (`#0D0D0E`) paired with chalk white (`#F4F4F2`) for pure editorial contrast.

- **Obsidian Coal (`#0D0D0E`):** Base canvas and primary structural fill. Absorbs ambient light and creates infinite depth.
- **Acid Lime (`#CCFF00`):** Primary signal color. Used for active states, playhead scrubbers, live tour status, interactive cursors, and visceral emphasis.
- **Vermilion Ember (`#FF3E14`):** Secondary tension accent. Reserved for sold-out badges, critical release stamps, waveform peaks, and secondary focal interactions.
- **Chalk White (`#F4F4F2`):** Primary text and hairline structural rules. Provides a raw paper-like contrast on deep obsidian.
- **Charcoal Muted (`#222225`):** Secondary surface elevation, track row dividers, and inactive waveform channels.
- **Ghost Metadata (`#76767E`):** Timecodes, catalog numbers, genre metadata, and micro-labels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on radical contrast between hyper-expressive display scales and utilitarian precision instruments. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Syne`):** Carries the sculptural weight of neoclassical monumentality combined with raw avant-garde curves. Used in all-caps or selective mixed-case for release titles, lyric snippets, artist branding, and section dividers. Tight line-height allows text to stack into monolithic physical slabs.
- **Body & Editorial Narratives (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides a clean, slightly technical grotesque feel that handles press pull quotes, curatorial statements, and long-form manifestos without fatigue.
- **Metadata & Technical Indices (`JetBrains Mono`):** Renders all track times, discography catalog IDs (e.g., `VV-048-LP`), BPM indicators, venue coordinates, audio sample rates, and tour timestamps. Always uppercase with loose letter spacing to anchor content with archival authority.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an uncompromising 12-column architectural grid on desktop (reflowing to 6 columns on tablet and 2 columns on mobile). Instead of standard floating containers, elements are partitioned via strict structural rules resembling an unyielding physical poster or technical data sheet.

- **Outer Margins:** Sizable canvas framing (`margin: 3rem`) isolates the portfolio from browser chrome, creating an intentional display frame.
- **Section Rhythm:** Vast vertical gaps (`space-xl` scaled to 6rem–10rem between chapters) establish deliberate stillness before erupting into massive typography.
- **Mobile Reflow:** On mobile viewports, columns condense into stacked full-bleed panels. Gaps drop down to hairline rules, transforming the interface into a continuous, scrolling archival feed.

## Elevation & Depth

This system decisively avoids skeuomorphic dropshadows, blurred ambient glows, or generic SaaS elevation models. Visual layering and spatial tension are achieved strictly through:

1. **High-Contrast Planar Overlaps:** Artwork, typography, and lyric banners overlap directly using 100% opacity or crisp exclusion blend modes.
2. **Hairline Structural Borders:** Crisp, razor-thin borders (`1px solid #222225` for subtle boundaries, `1px solid #F4F4F2` or `1px solid #CCFF00` for active focal sections) dictate depth and modular grouping.
3. **Negative Space Voids:** Deep `#0D0D0E` acts as absolute negative space; elevated interactive planes use subtle tonal fills (`#151518`) with razor borders rather than drop shadows.
4. **Kinetic Waveform Strips:** Waveform canvases and audio visualizer nodes cut directly through content planes with zero perimeter feathering.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is strictly unapologetic and angular: `roundedness: 0`.

- Every button, card, audio scrubber, album sleeve container, and badge uses sharp 90-degree corners.
- Circular elements are used exclusively for physical audio metaphors (spindles, circular vinyl progress rings, and playhead knobs).
- Tag pills are replaced by rectangular technical tags enclosed in crisp 1px borders with monospaced index codes.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (High-Contrast Invert):** Rectangular solid chalk-white fill (`#F4F4F2`) with obsidian typography (`#0D0D0E`) in all-caps `JetBrains Mono`. Hover flips to electric Acid Lime (`#CCFF00`) with instant 0ms transition.
- **Ghost Action:** Obsidian fill with 1px border of `#F4F4F2`. Hover shifts border and text to `#CCFF00`.
- **Audio Play/Pause Trigger:** Monolithic block button featuring an oversized play icon paired with real-time running millisecond timecode (`00:00:00`).

### Discography & Tracklist Rows
- **List Structure:** Full-bleed horizontal rows bordered by top and bottom 1px `#222225` rules.
- **Information Hierarchy:** Left: catalog sequence (`01`, `02`) in oversized `label-numeral`; Center: track title in `headline-md` paired with a mini live SVG waveform; Right: duration, key signature, and format tags in `label-code`.
- **Hover/Active State:** Entire row background shifts to `#151518` with track title text flashing to `#CCFF00`.

### Tour Date Ledger
- **Architecture:** Tabular layout with colossal dates (Day number in `headline-xl`, month in `label-code`).
- **Venue & City:** Rendered in `headline-md` with geolocation coordinates in ghost metadata.
- **Status Indicator:** Tickets active displays a stark Acid Lime button (`TICKETS`); sold out states are stamped with a vermilion stroke badge (`SOLD OUT / REDISTRIBUTION ONLY`).

### Lyric & Pull-Quote Kinetic Billboard
- **Structure:** Edge-to-edge horizontally scrolling ticker or stacked massive display typography spanning 100vw.
- **Styling:** Alternating filled text (`#F4F4F2`) and outlined/stroke text (`-webkit-text-stroke: 1px #F4F4F2`), intermittently slashed with `#FF3E14` or `#CCFF00` annotations.

### Media & Artwork Cards
- **Container:** Pure zero-radius box with 1px outer rule. High-contrast monochromatic photography that transitions to full hyper-vibrant color strictly on hover.
- **Caption Block:** Placed directly beneath the asset with zero margin, separated by a 1px divider, containing release year, production credits, and track count in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Audio Player Dock
- **Format:** Persistent bottom docking bar in solid obsidian (`#0D0D0E`) with a top border in `#CCFF00`.
- **Waveform Scrubber:** Raw segmented bar visualizer; completed segments illuminate in Acid Lime, unplayed segments remain Charcoal (`#222225`).
